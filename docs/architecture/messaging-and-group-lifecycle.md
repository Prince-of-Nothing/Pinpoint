# Messaging and Group Lifecycle

## Direct messaging

Messaging is a Full-mode feature for trusted connections, with delivery state,
mute, block, report, deletion, and optional disappearing messages.

## Groups

A user can request a temporary group based on goal, interest, perspective, and
availability, or create a group with rules and roles. Members can preview the
purpose, accept or leave, remain pseudonymous, and report abuse.

Random discovery is isolated from location sharing. Group membership never grants
precise location access.

## Challenges in chats

A group chat can host a challenge as a structured activity with its own prompt,
deadline, submissions, scoring, participant list, and results thread. The chat
remains the discussion and invitation space. Leaving the chat or challenge must be
handled separately, and a challenge must not silently retain a member's
participation after they withdraw. See
[Friend Challenges and Group Prompts](../explanation/friend-challenges-and-group-prompts.md).
