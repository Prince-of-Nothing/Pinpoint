# Scope and MVP Boundaries

## Essential

- Android and iPhone support.
- Trusted friend groups and duration-based or continuous live location sharing.
- OpenStreetMap-based map with freshness and accuracy indicators.
- Built-in routing or export to another installed maps app.
- Location caches with named-friend or area-based access, expiry, revocation, and
  reporting.
- Consent, stop-sharing, blocking, deletion, privacy, and abuse controls.

## Optional or later

- Direct messaging, voice calls, and video calls.
- Interest- and availability-based matching with random group chats.
- Creator-led groups with rules and moderation.
- Daily prompts, media sharing, ratings, discovery, and prompt suggestions.
- Coding challenges and other creative prompt activities.
- Private safety, ethics, or vulnerability reports with optional anonymity and
  status tracking.

Every optional feature gets its own permissions and moderation rules; joining a
group must never grant location access.

See [Social and Community Features](../explanation/social-and-community-features.md)
and [Creative and Playful Features](../explanation/creative-and-playful-features.md)
for detail on the optional/later feature set, and
