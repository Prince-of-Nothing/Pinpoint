# Friend Challenges and Group Prompts

## Concept

Friends can turn shared interests into small challenges: invite people,
submit something, compare results, and have fun together. Challenges are
social activities inside a known group, not anonymous competitions by
default.

## Challenge types

- photo, drawing, writing, audio, video, or code prompts;
- map or cache scavenger hunts;
- prediction, trivia, puzzle, or quiz rounds;
- fitness or exploration goals when participants deliberately opt in;
- collaborative challenges where the group wins together;
- friendly head-to-head challenges with a clear end time.

## Group-chat connection

A challenge can be created inside an existing group chat or attached to a
global prompt. The group chat supplies the initial participant list,
discussion space, invitations, reminders, and results thread. Members can
accept, decline, leave, or join before the challenge locks.

Global prompts remain discoverable, but participation is organized through a
selected group chat, creator group, or explicitly public event. This keeps
the participant list clear and prevents an anonymous crowd from appearing
around a user's content without consent.

## Rules and results

Every challenge defines its prompt, participants, deadline, submission
format, scoring method, visibility, moderation rules, and whether there is a
winner. Scores may be peer ratings, objective answers, creator judging,
completion, or cooperative progress. The creator can choose a playful title
instead of ranking people.

Participant lists use approved display names or nicknames. Exact location,
private profile data, and friend relationships outside the group are never
revealed by a challenge.

## Safety

Creators can cancel, edit, moderate, remove entries, and report abuse.
Challenges must not pressure users to disclose live location, sensitive
information, or unsafe activities. Location-based challenges use coarse
eligibility or cache rules rather than exposing participant trails. See
[Cache and Permission Model](../architecture/cache-and-permission-model.md).
