# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project has an extra duty: it handles **location data and personal safety**. Treat
discussions about real users' privacy with the seriousness the subject deserves. Jokes or
hypotheticals that normalize stalking, covert tracking, or doxxing are not acceptable here.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive, and kind; assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give credit; cite sources and prior work (this is academic context).
- Keep discussions focused on building a safer product.
- Use inclusive language and accessible communication.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, trolling, personal or political attacks, public or private intimidation.
- Sharing others' private information (real names, addresses, locations, messages)
  without explicit permission — especially egregious given the domain of this app.
- Soliciting, demonstrating, or assisting with covert tracking or abuse tooling.
- Publishing vulnerability details before a fix window (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Sustained disruption of reviews, discussions, or contributions.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions
that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainer(s) via the private contact channels listed in `SECURITY.md`. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and confidentiality will be respected
for reporters.

## Enforcement guidelines
1. **Correction** — private written warning, clarification of why the behavior was harmful.
2. **Temporary warning** — no interaction with those affected for a defined period.
3. **Temporary ban** — time-limited removal from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — sustained violations or severe single incidents (e.g., doxxing).
